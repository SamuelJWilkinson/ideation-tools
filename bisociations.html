Bisociations

1. Get your words

Generate random nouns, or hand the page to a friend and have them type in words you haven't seen. 🔒 holds a word in place; 🎲 swaps just that one.

    2. Force the collision

    Take each word in turn and smash it into your design. Write without stopping and without editing: associations, jokes, half-sentences, nonsense. When the stream runs dry, pull out the idea it left behind.

    3. Your ideas

    Star the ones with legs, then start another round: carry a starred idea forward as the new thing to design and run the words through it again.
